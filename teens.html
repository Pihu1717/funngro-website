GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

A few quick answers to help you understand the idea.

Do I need to be an expert already? +

No. This concept is about exploring your interests, practising skills and learning as you go. Start with what you know and stay open to learning.

What kind of skills can I explore? +

You can explore creative, communication, research and technology-related skills. Your interests can change as you discover new things.

How should I choose what to try first? +

Choose something you are curious about, set a small goal and give it a try. A small first step is better than waiting for the perfect plan.
